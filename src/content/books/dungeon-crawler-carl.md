reviewText: |
  The Apocalypse will be telivised
  Dungeon Crawler Carl opens on the worst day in human history: the surface of the Earth is wiped out for an alien game show, and the survivors are dropped into an eighteen-floor dungeon to fight for the cameras. Carl, still in his boxers, goes in with his ex-girlfriend's prize-winning show cat, Princess Donut, who soon has a lot to say for herself.

  What hooked me straight away was the voice. Carl is funny, stubborn and decent, and the jokes never stop him caring about the people around him. My favourite character, though, is the System AI, whose achievement messages are so petty and rude that I laughed out loud more than once; "Mana Toast. This is toast. It refills your mana. That's it." sums it up perfectly. Under the crude humour there's a sharper story about entertainment that profits from suffering, and a line like "If we get to the point where we don't help each other anymore, that's when we stop being human" lands harder than you'd expect from a book with a talking cat in it.

  It isn't perfect. The early chapters lean on a lot of game rules, stat boxes and loot lists, and the humour is crude enough that it won't be for everyone. If you enjoy LitRPG, dark comedy or just a story that's very hard to put down, this is the place to start. It's the beginning of one of the most engaging stories I've read, and it goes straight onto the Must Read shelf.
