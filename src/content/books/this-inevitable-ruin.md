Carl and Donut become warlords in the Faction Wars, leading their own side in a full-scale war, and a crowd of past crawlers joins their team, including some of the authors of the cookbook. True to the verdict, everyone comes together to commit a horrific amount of war crimes.

This is the series at its most ambitious, and it pulls it off. Seeing characters from the past finally stand beside Carl and Donut, especially the cookbook authors whose notes have guided them for so long, felt like a reward for every book that came before. My favourite character here is Prepotente, who is as unpredictable and entertaining as ever and steals every scene he's in. The book also has some of the series' wisest lines, like "When Big Things happen on such a large scale, it's easy to forget sometimes that these Big Things are also happening to the little things in the world." And Donut reminding everyone that "It's okay to be a work in progress" is exactly the kind of warmth that keeps the darkness bearable.

With so many factions, allies and battles to keep straight, it's a lot to take in, so it helps to have the earlier books fresh in your mind. But it's huge, emotional and endlessly fun, and it's a Must Read that sits right at the top of the series for me.
